Indicators and Buttons

The S5735-L8T4X-QA-V2 has similar indicators to those on the S5735-L8P2T4X-A-V2 except that the S5735-L8T4X-QA-V2 does not have a PoE mode indicator. For details, see the S5735-L8P2T4X-A-V2.

Power Supply System

The switch has a built-in AC power module and does not support pluggable power modules.

Heat Dissipation System

The switch has no fans and uses natural heat dissipation.
