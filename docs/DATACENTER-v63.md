# Datacenter v63

- Replaced the overly conservative spherical overview fit with a perspective-aware cylindrical envelope, retaining room bounds throughout rotation. Lower initial elevation brings the model closer and makes more use of desktop viewport space. Horizontal and vertical field of view still constrain portrait framing.
- Installed equipment can be dragged vertically within its original rack. Pointer capture keeps a drag active outside the canvas; a six-pixel threshold distinguishes a click from a drag. Holding within a multi-U chassis preserves the grabbed U offset. A translucent green/red landing ghost uses exact contiguous-U validation. Other racks, overlap, out-of-bounds targets and off-canvas drops do not commit. Escape, pointer cancellation and lost focus cancel. Existing item identity, model, serial and notes are preserved; committed moves use existing persistence and undo/redo.
- Ordinary clicking and double-click inspection remain available. Camera orbit and walking input pause during the drag. Mouse Look must be unlocked for pointer-based manipulation.
- Facility cabinet appearance follows inspected Schneider Galaxy VL and Vertiv Liebert PDX/PCW exterior references. See `research/facility-reference-v63.json`. Scene models are conceptual external enclosures, not calibrated vendor CAD, electrical sizing or thermal simulation. Their placement/collision footprints remain those of the existing sample room.

Validation includes closer overview distance versus v62, portrait fit, existing auto rotation/pause, real Three.js raycasting for installed-device pointer drag and target U, fixed camera while dragging, Escape cancellation, multi-U overlap/boundary rejection, self-overlap and preservation of metadata. The renderer and DOM are mocked in automated scene tests; actual browser/GPU performance and touch behavior are not verified in this environment.
