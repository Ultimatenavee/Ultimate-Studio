Ragnarok directional occupants

Original artwork: Ragnarok Online / Gravity. Source SPR/ACT archive paths and
SHA-256 hashes are recorded in manifest.json. Generated by
assets-source/ayothaya/export_life.py using the configured local client and ragx.
Rebuild with: uv run --python 3.12 --with pillow --with shapely python
assets-source/ayothaya/export_life.py
Visitors need only the hosted atlases and manifest, not a game installation.

The atlas retains original indexed/RGBA pixels, ACT transforms, directional clips
and delays (ACT delay units * 25 ms). Identical composed frames share cells;
animation clip lengths/timing remain intact. One shared origin/ground anchor
across all directions preserves body proportions and original bounce/gestures.

The runtime replaces the capital GLB's 10 static SPR_ material batches with 120
camera-facing, independently animated occupants: 42 NPCs and 78 monsters.
There are 70 additional monsters across 14 neighborhoods, including eight MVP
landmarks and 11 mini bosses in the complete population. The observed side selects the
original pose, with angular hysteresis to avoid view-boundary flicker. Ground
shadows are instanced per parent area. Indoor scientists use the same system.

NPCs retain source idle gestures. Placement headings face a specified road or
forecourt. A nearby walker attracts their attention with a bounded turning speed
and distance hysteresis; leaving restores the assigned heading. Overview and
drone cameras do not direct the NPCs' physical facing.

assets-source/ayothaya/city_life_plan.py defines the neighborhood themes and
selects complete clear habitats using source terrain, building/prop envelopes,
the replacement lab parcels, reserved avenues and portal approaches. Wildlife
prefers grass, while engineering creatures can use paved side plazas. The full
roaming territory and sprite width are reserved, not just the center point.
Each movement step checks buildings, nearby furniture and exact surface coverage.
Large MVP landmarks animate in place; smaller creatures alternate idle/walk
clips within their neighborhood. Animation pause freezes the simulation.
